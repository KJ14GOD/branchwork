The activity chart adds up to more than the number of entries in the log: an entry stamped exactly midnight is counted on the day that just ended as well as on the day that is starting. The failing cases are "an entry stamped exactly midnight belongs to the day that is starting" and "the chart's total is the number of entries, not more".
